# Player guide

## Your first minutes
1. **Choose a class and birthsign** — the character creation screen opens when you first join
   (reopen with `/ur class choose`). Your class sets starting skills, gear and coin.
2. **Follow "A New Beginning"** (journal: **J**) — find a village and speak with its Elder.
3. Check the **notice board** in every village for bounties and supply orders.

## Keys
| Key | |
|---|---|
| **K** | Skills, perks, character level and attribute choices |
| **J** | Quest journal (track / abandon quests) |
| **G** | Cast your readied spell |
| **H** | Ready the next spell |
| **N** | Spellbook — put known spells into your 5 quick slots |
| Use NPC | Talk (dialogue options: click or press 1–9) |
| Sneak + use NPC | Pickpocket |

## Skills (1–99)
You get better at what you do. **Combat**: One-Handed, Two-Handed, Archery, Block, Heavy Armor,
Smithing. **Magic**: Destruction, Restoration, Alteration, Conjuration, Illusion, Enchanting.
**Stealth**: Light Armor, Sneak, Lockpicking, Pickpocket, Speech, Alchemy. **Artisan**: Mining,
Woodcutting, Fishing, Farming, Cooking, Agility.

Every skill level raises your **character level**; each character level gives a **perk point** and
an **attribute choice** (Health, Magicka or Stamina) — spend them in the Skills screen (K).
Better gear and ores need skill levels (shown in tooltips), RuneScape style.

## Vitals
* **Stamina** (bottom right) drains when sprinting, jumping and swinging; exhausted fighters hit softer and can't sprint.
* **Magicka** (bottom left) powers spells. Both regenerate after a short pause. Potions of Restore Magicka/Stamina help.

## Money
**Crowns** are your currency. Coins you pick up go in your purse when used. Your balance shows under
the inventory. Merchants' prices improve with Speech. You lose a little coin when you die.

## Magic
Read **spell tomes** (bought from court wizards and priests, found in chests) to learn spells; you need
enough skill in the spell's school. Ready spells in the spellbook (N) and cast with G.

## Townsfolk
Elders, guard captains, court wizards, merchants, smiths, innkeepers, priests and more. Many sell
goods, some **train skills** (five times per character level), and many have quests. Guards defend
towns. Getting caught pickpocketing costs a fine — and their goodwill.
