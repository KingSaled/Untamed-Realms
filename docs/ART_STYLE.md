# Untamed Realms art style

The design language for every sprite, texture and model we add (UI has its own rules in
`tools/artforge/assets/ui.py`). Everything is generated by ArtForge from code, and
`python3 -m artforge check` (run by CI) enforces the rules marked **[lint]**, so art that breaks
them never ships.

Preview sheets: [`docs/art/`](art/).

## 1. Fit vanilla, then go one step richer
* **Resolution:** items, blocks and GUI icons are **16×16**; entity skins **64×64**; armor layers
  **64×32**. No HD textures and no mixed texel sizes on one model. **[lint: size]**
* **Hard pixels:** every pixel is fully opaque or fully transparent. No anti-aliasing, no soft
  shadows. Glow is shown with colour, not alpha. **[lint: alpha]**
* **Readable at 1×:** check every sprite at 100% zoom on the hotbar. The silhouette alone must
  say what the item is.

## 2. Light, shading and outline
* **Light comes from the top-left.** Surfaces facing up-left take the light shades, those facing
  down-right the dark ones. Applies to sprites, medallions and model faces alike.
* **Ramps:** every material is a 5-shade ramp, dark → light (`artforge/palette.py`). Shadows drift
  toward cool and purple, highlights toward warm, so materials never look grey and flat. Use
  index 4 (or `w`) only for small specular glints.
* **Outline:** silhouettes get a 1px outline in `OUTLINE` (#1b1420, a dark plum) or `OUTLINE_SOFT`
  for inner separations. Never pure black. **[lint: outline]**
* **Palette only:** colours come from the palette ramps plus `OUTLINE`, `OUTLINE_SOFT`, `INK`
  (writing) and white. A new material means a new ramp in `palette.py`, never an ad-hoc colour.
  **[lint: palette]**
* **Colour budget:** at most 16 colours per item and 12 per icon. **[lint: colors]**

## 3. Composition
* **Icons are centred.** Medallion-style icons (spells, abilities, perks) are symmetric about the
  canvas centre (7.5, 7.5). Their glyphs sit on an **even-sized grid** (8×8), and the glyph's
  drawn pixels must span a box centred in that grid. This is what keeps the symbol optically
  centred on the medallion. **[lint: centered]**, and `lint.centered_glyph()` refuses to build a
  glyph that breaks it.
* **Weapons are diagonal**, pointing from bottom-left (grip) to top-right (tip), like vanilla
  swords. Bows face right. Every weapon type keeps the same grip position, so a whole hotbar of
  weapons lines up.
* **Armor icons** are front-facing and centred, with the same shoulder height across a set.
* Leave at least a 1px transparent margin unless the shape must touch the edge (long blades).

## 4. Families: one template, many materials
Each family is one ASCII template rendered with different ramps (`artforge/sprite.py`), so a tier
set is consistent by construction:

| Family | Template slots | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Weapons | blade = primary ramp, grip = secondary (leather/wood), guard & pommel = tertiary (accent metal) | one template per weapon type; tiers swap ramps |
| Armor icons | plate = primary, straps/trim = secondary, accent = tertiary | light vs heavy have different silhouettes |
| Armor layers | painted per model face from the same ramps, lit top-left | `assets/arsenal.py` |
| Potions | liquid = primary, cork = wood | glass highlight `w` top-left |
| Tomes / books | cover = school / material ramp, pages = parchment | |
| Spell icons | school ramp medallion + glyph | `G.medallion_icon` |
| Jewelry | band = metal ramp, stone = gem ramp | ring stones always at the top |

### Material tiers (Skyrim order)
Iron → Steel → Orcish → Dwarven → Elven → Glass → Ebony → Daedric. Every tier is a palette ramp with
its own hue. Higher tiers get a more saturated, more contrasted ramp, and the accent metal shifts
(iron/leather → bronze → gold → ebony/red) so tiers read apart at a glance.

### Magic schools
Destruction = fire orange, Restoration = gold, Alteration = violet-blue, Conjuration = purple,
Illusion = teal. Anything school-related (tomes, icons, spell effects) uses its school's ramp.

## 5. 3D models (blocks, stations)
* Cuboids on the 16-unit grid (`artforge/model3d.py`). Texture density **1 texel = 1/16 block** on
  every face, so stretched faces are forbidden.
* Same top-left lighting baked into textures. Top faces use the light end of the ramp.
* Stations (forge, tanning rack, alchemy lab, arcane enchanter) share one construction kit: a dark
  wood frame (`WOOD_DARK`), iron fittings (`IRON`), and one signature material per station.

## 6. Adding an asset
1. Pick the family and ramps (add a ramp to `palette.py` if the material is new).
2. Add the template or the generator call in `tools/artforge/assets/`.
3. `python3 -m artforge build`, look at `docs/art/`, and fix anything `python3 -m artforge lint`
   reports.
4. Commit the generated files together with the code. CI fails if they drift apart.
