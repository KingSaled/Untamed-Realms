# ur-arcana

Skyrim's alchemy and enchanting.

* **Alchemy lab** (`AlchemyScreen`, `alchemy/`): ingredients (`data/<ns>/urarcana/ingredients`) each have four
  effects (`data/<ns>/urarcana/alchemy_effects`). Tasting reveals the first; brewing 2-3 ingredients makes
  every shared effect and reveals it. The most valuable effect names the brew; harmful = thrown poison.
  Strength follows Alchemy and the `alchemy_power` perk effect.
* **Arcane enchanter** (`EnchanterScreen`, `enchanting/`): disenchant to learn enchantments (vanilla's
  data-driven ones, and any mod's), enchant gear with a known enchantment and a filled soul gem.
* **Soul gems** fill when a creature dies under the `soul_trapped` effect, applied by the Soul Trap spell
  (`data/urarcana/urmagic/spells/soul_trap.json`, read by ur-magic).
* Knowledge is a player attachment (`ArcanaKnowledge`), synced to the client for the windows and tooltips.
* `/ur arcana learnall|fillgems|forget <player>` for testing.

Content is generated by `tools/datagen/arcana_content.py`; art by `tools/artforge/assets/arcana.py`.
